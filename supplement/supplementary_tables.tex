\title{Supplementary Tables for ``Attribution-Constrained SMOTE: A Controlled Evaluation of Explanation-Guided Oversampling for Imbalanced Classification}
\author{Uday Raj and Monika Sharma}\date{}\maketitle
\noindent All tables are computed from the per-fold results released with the code (dataset-level means over 15 folds; $N=10$ datasets). AC-SMOTE is compared with each baseline by a two-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test. Holm and Benjamini--Hochberg (BH) adjustments are applied within a family of seven comparisons (one setting, one metric). Median diff.\ is the median dataset-level difference (AC-SMOTE minus baseline); $r_{rb}$ is the matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation. Settings: LR = Logistic Regression on raw features; LR-std = standardized features (exploratory, seed 42).
\section*{S1. Equivalence tests: AC-SMOTE versus SMOTE}
\noindent TOST: two one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests against margins $\pm\delta$; the reported $p$ is the larger of the two, Holm-adjusted across the four settings. CI: 95\% percentile bootstrap interval of the median (20\,000 resamples). Min.\ det.\ diff.: smallest difference detectable at 80\% power by a paired $t$-test with the observed standard deviation of the differences, $N=10$ (an approximation). Margins were chosen after the data were seen.
